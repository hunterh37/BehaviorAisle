Behavior Aisle is a full-scale grocery store on Apple Vision Pro, stocked with simulated shoppers. BehaviorGPT, Unbox AI's large behavioral model, predicts each shopper's next action, and each shopper then walks, looks and buys based on that prediction. A shopper can start from a real X profile, so a home cook who runs heads for sourdough, tomatoes and yogurt. Every shopper shows what it will probably do next and the reason.

A merchandiser can move a product, add a sale sign or empty a shelf, and the app replays the same 2,000 shoppers in the old store and the changed store. It reports the result as gross margin per store per week, with a 95% confidence interval and a chain-wide yearly total. It also shows units taken from other products and the share of shoppers who walk away when an item is out of stock. One example: a 20% chip promotion nearly doubled chip sales and still cost $2,595 in margin per store per week. A buyer sees that before reset night, not after weeks of sales data.
