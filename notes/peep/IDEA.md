# Shelfcast

Immersive grocery store where BehaviorGPT shoppers walk, decide and buy on Apple Vision Pro.

Origin: Sundai Hack 148 at MIT (8 Nov 2026), theme Large Behavioral Models, with Unbox AI's BehaviorGPT-v4.
BehaviorGPT takes a person's recent actions (searches, views, purchases) and predicts the next one. Behavior
Aisle gives those predictions bodies: every SKU is a RealityHD prop on a shelf and every prediction is a HumanHD
character walking to it.

Use: merchandisers test shelf moves and promotions on predicted shoppers before moving a real shelf. The
Vision Pro adds the 1:1 walkthrough that a planogram spreadsheet cannot show.

Features: immersive lifesize store, 5 persona shoppers, top-3 prediction cards, probability arcs, fork view
with ghost branches, drag and sale-sign what-if, 50-shopper heatmap, checkout anomaly badge, live, replay and
offline prediction sources.

Target feel: standing in a real supermarket aisle, watching people make up their minds.

Core loop: predict, walk, pick, re-predict. The user changes a shelf or price; shoppers re-route.

First reward: after a long-press sale sign, a shopper's arc swings to that shelf and it turns and walks there.

Platforms: visionOS 26 (Apple Vision Pro).

Full requirements: PRD.md.
