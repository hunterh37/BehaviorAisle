# Behavior Aisle
Idea: test store layouts and promotions on predicted shoppers at 1:1 scale on Apple Vision Pro.
Features: immersive store, HumanHD shoppers, BehaviorGPT predictions, fork view, what-if, heatmap, anomaly.
Target feel: standing in a real supermarket aisle watching shoppers decide.
Core loop: predict, walk, pick, re-predict. User changes shelf or price, shoppers reroute.
First reward: arcs shift and a shopper walks to the product just moved.
Platforms: visionOS 26.
