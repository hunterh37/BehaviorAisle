ehavior Aisle: real-data validation and plan test

Validation (tools/validate_instacart.py, Instacart train split, 600 held-out basket prefixes)
Task: given the SKUs added to the cart so far, predict the next SKU (32 SKUs).

Model              hit@1   hit@3   MRR
Random             2.5%    9.3%    0.127
Popularity         7.7%    23.8%   0.231
Co-occurrence      8.7%    25.2%   0.242
Hand-set heuristic 2.7%    10.5%   0.139
Fitted heuristic   8.3%    25.2%   0.240
BehaviorGPT        4.7%    12.5%   0.163

BehaviorGPT minus hand-set heuristic, hit@3: +2.0 pts, 95% CI -1.7 to +6.0 (no significant gain).
Fitted minus hand-set heuristic, hit@3: +14.7 pts, 95% CI +10.3 to +19.3.

Reading: the original heuristic is near random on real baskets. BehaviorGPT through the shop/recommend
endpoint is below plain popularity on this task. Adding popularity and co-occurrence fitted on real
baskets brings the heuristic level with the best baseline. Shelf height, sale and distance effects still
have no real data behind them.

  and discount cost of sale items.

- UI/PlanTestView.swift: "Plan test" panel (revenue, items/basket, meters, discount, top SKU shifts)
  and "Real-data check" panel (hit@3 bars, CIs) in the main window.
