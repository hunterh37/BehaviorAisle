PITCH (one line)
Test a store change before moving a single shelf.

PITCH (10 seconds)
Shelfcast is a 3D twin of your store. AI shoppers, driven by
BehaviorGPT, walk it the way real customers would. Move a product or
put it on sale, and you see the new paths before you change the real store.

PITCH (30 seconds)
Store owners rearrange shelves on gut feel and find out weeks later if it
worked. Shelfcast is a 3D copy of the store filled with simulated
shoppers. Each shopper's next move comes from BehaviorGPT, a model trained
on real shopping sequences, so they pick products based on what they
already looked at and have in their cart. Drag an item to eye level or put
it on sale and the shoppers' paths, carts and checkout timing change on
screen. The owner sees the effect of a layout change before anyone moves a box.

TRUTH CHECKS (if a judge pushes)
- Shoppers are simulated, not tracked customers. No cameras, no personal data.
- BehaviorGPT ranks which product each shopper picks next.
- Shelf height and sale boosts are app-side weights; BehaviorGPT has no
  input for layout.
- It is a fast what-if tool. It does not replace a real A/B test.
